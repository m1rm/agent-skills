---
name: grill-me
description: A relentless interview to sharpen a plan or design.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Help the user sharpen the current plan or design through a focused interview.
Read the available context first, then ask one concrete question at a time about
the most consequential unresolved assumption, tradeoff, failure mode, or success
criterion. Challenge vague answers without inventing requirements. Explain the
tradeoff when useful, and stop when the design is decision-ready or the user asks.
Summarize decisions and remaining uncertainties. Do not implement anything or
require another skill or a harness-specific question tool.
